# Writing Plans (excerpt)

## Plan shape
Each step: exact files to touch, the change, how to verify. If a step cannot
name its verification, it is not a step yet.

## Batching
Batch steps into commits worth shipping independently. A plan that ends in one
giant commit was not a plan.
