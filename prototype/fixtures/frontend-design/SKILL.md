# Frontend Design (excerpt)

## Aesthetic direction
Commit to a bold aesthetic direction before writing code. Pick a domain,
typography pairing, and color system; avoid default Tailwind blues and
Inter-as-identity.

## Typography
Choose characterful fonts. Pair a display face with a workhorse body face;
tune tracking on headings, never on body text.

## Motion
Motion is hierarchy: staggered reveals, 150–200ms easings, hover states that
respond within one frame. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
